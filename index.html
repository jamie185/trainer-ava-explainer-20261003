Every change has a reason

The same reason list JF Coach uses, so spares and pay stay right. Ava only offers the ones that fit the notice (under 12 hours, or a before-11am session after 6pm the night before, is late).

Book

  • Regular session from their package
  • Using a spare (she checks the spare balance first)
  • Signup bonus or Google review session
  • Referral stays in JF Coach (needs the referred person)
  • Kickstarters get the kickstart session type automatically

Move

  • Client asked, with enough notice
  • Client asked late, still moving it
  • Trainer had to move it
  • Booked at the wrong time

Cancel

  • Client cancelled with notice (she offers to rebook first)
  • Client cancelled late: stays booked by default, your 12-hour rule
  • Trainer cancelled, kept as a spare
  • Booked by mistake, or a client who's finished PT
  • Free sessions go to the gym's VA

Locked the same way, plus more

Your simple rule: anything a trainer can see in JF Coach, the portal or JF Notes, Ava can help with; anything they can't, she can't.

1

Their clients only

Checked on the server for every read, message, note and booking, not by the model.

2

A yes for anything outward

Client messages, JF Notes posts and cancels need the trainer's yes to the exact text.

3

No money, no pay

No prices, payments or payslips. Messages with amounts, links or phone numbers are blocked.

4

Data is data

Instructions hidden in client chats, JF Notes or notes are ignored. Tested with planted ones.

How it was tested

Sol agents wrote 348+ realistic multi-step chats in each trainer's real voice (from 13 months of their WhatsApps), ran them with the real Ava model against a copy of the live data (no messages to anyone), then graded every chat as that trainer. Three rounds of fix and re-test, plus three code reviews (security, scheduling, the problem loop). Final average 8.5/10; leaks found in round one (2) were fixed and the retest round had none.

What the testing caught and fixed

  • Payment notes on a client's JF Notes card could reach a trainer: now hidden before Ava sees them.
  • A "yes" quoting an older, replaced check could approve the newer one: now refused.
  • A trainer could slip an @tag for another gym's VA into a note: now blocked, Ava adds the only tag.
  • One client "yes" could have booked two different offers: only the latest offer counts, matched by message order.
  • Overnight rest was miscounted across tonight's daylight saving change, and admin blocks and other trainers' bookings were invisible to the scheduler: all fixed.
  • Finn's number in the roster (from HubSpot) was wrong, so his messages would have been refused: fixed to his real 0401 871 846.
